# MACS Phase 05 Creative Territory Lab

Status: prototype/design exploration only — not production selection

Quality bar: COLLINS-level rigor, not COLLINS visual imitation.

Design doctrine:
- strategy and proof before styling;
- real content before containers;
- minimum necessary interface around maximum useful capability;
- three divergent territories before final selection;
- user outcome, clarity and trust outrank visual novelty;
- real founder/work material over generic SaaS imagery;
- owner selects one territory before broad Phase 06 implementation.

## Shared story spine

All territories preserve the approved six-beat architecture:

1. Point of view
2. Programs
3. Work
4. Father + son
5. Built Here
6. Fit

They differ materially in governing idea, grid, typography behavior, image treatment, pacing, motion, navigation expression and signature moment.

## Territory A — LONG VIEW

Class: restrained

Governing idea: **The long view.**

MACS is a small Northwest firm built around durable relationships, clear ownership and calm judgment. The site feels like an editorial field journal rather than a software company.

- Grid: generous editorial columns, long horizontal rules, restrained asymmetry.
- Type: large humanist/editorial serif + quiet sans utility text.
- Color: warm paper, ink, MACS blue; coral only as signal/action.
- Imagery: documentary founder/client photography at large scale.
- Motion: almost none; simple media crossfades and responsive feedback.
- Navigation: minimal top line/menu.
- Signature moment: full-bleed founder image followed by a silent one-line credibility strip.
- Accessibility risk: large editorial type must preserve mobile line breaks and contrast.
- Performance risk: photography/video payload.
- Imitation risk: low if imagery and layout stay rooted in MACS rather than copying a reference composition.
- Commercial advantage: trust, maturity, selectivity.

## Territory B — TWO CLOCKS

Class: expressive

Governing idea: **One watches what has to last. One stays close to what is changing.**

The father/son difference becomes the composition system. Stacy and Stavarai are not matching co-founder cards; they are two complementary operating clocks.

- Grid: two unequal columns that periodically exchange dominance.
- Type: one measured editorial voice paired with a sharper contemporary system voice.
- Color: ink/paper/blue with coral time-marker accents and neutral gray.
- Imagery: paired crops and business/creator contrasts.
- Motion: a single moving time-marker/column handoff on key transitions; reduced-motion becomes a static split.
- Navigation: clear horizontal menu with a subtle dual-rail indicator.
- Signature moment: father and son imagery separates, then resolves into one shared frame as the story reaches the MACS promise.
- Accessibility risk: split layouts must collapse into a deliberate reading order on mobile.
- Performance risk: moderate if using paired video; default to stills.
- Imitation risk: low; the governing concept is specific to this founder pairing.
- Commercial advantage: makes the generational combination memorable and commercially legible.

## Territory C — CONFLUENCE

Class: experimental

Governing idea: **Two currents. One accountable team.**

The Pacific Northwest is expressed through confluence rather than clichés: two streams of expertise merge into one operating path. The MACS angular mark becomes a structural source for linework, crops and transitions.

- Grid: flowing but bounded section offsets; content follows two visual rails that merge at proof/fit moments.
- Type: restrained serif/sans pairing; experimental composition comes from space and rails, not illegible typography.
- Color: MACS blue, warm paper, ink, gray, coral signal. No generic purple/blue gradients.
- Imagery: founder/client documentary media combined with flat vector current/horizon source art derived from MACS geometry.
- Motion: rails converge/diverge to orient narrative; no scroll hijacking.
- Navigation: conventional labels, visually tied to the current-line motif.
- Signature moment: two source lines travel independently through Programs/Work, merge around the father+son story, and continue as one line into Fit.
- Accessibility risk: decorative rails must never carry essential information and must disappear cleanly under reduced motion/high zoom.
- Performance risk: low when source art remains inline/light SVG.
- Imitation risk: low if the line system stays derived from MACS geometry and real Northwest story.
- Commercial advantage: most ownable/memorable territory without exposing technical complexity.

## Recommendation

Prototype all three. Current recommendation to test hardest: **TWO CLOCKS**, because the governing idea is directly tied to the black-swan commercial story and is difficult for a competitor to copy honestly.

Do not silently blend the territories. Owner selection is the Phase 05 exit gate.
