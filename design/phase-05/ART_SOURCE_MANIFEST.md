# MACS Phase 05 Art Source Manifest

All files below are original flat-vector studies created for MACS Phase 05 and use the approved MACS palette. They are source assets, not final logo replacements.

## Brand colors

- Warm paper: `#F6F6E9`
- Ink: `#171512`
- MACS blue: `#3A6EA5`
- Signal coral: `#FE6E60`
- Structural gray: `#B4B4B4`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Source files

### `public/art/macs-long-view-horizon.svg`
Territory: Long View
Purpose: restrained Northwest horizon/field-line graphic for editorial pacing, section transitions, print/social extensions, and motion studies.
Rules: may be recolored only within the approved palette; keep flat fills/strokes; no gradients or faux depth.

### `public/art/macs-two-clocks.svg`
Territory: Two Clocks
Purpose: generational operating metaphor — long-term business judgment + what is changing now.
Rules: clocks/rails are symbolic, not literal scheduling UI; the coral center is the shared MACS decision point.

### `public/art/macs-confluence.svg`
Territory: Confluence
Purpose: two currents merge into one accountable client path. The angular convergence is designed to harmonize with the MACS mark without becoming another logo.
Rules: decorative only; never use line color/position as the sole carrier of information.

## Production rules

- Preserve SVG as editable source.
- Export PNG/WebP derivatives only from these sources; do not raster-edit the master and call it canonical.
- Do not embed external raster images into the SVG masters.
- No gradients by default; the owner-supplied design protocol explicitly blacklists generic purple/blue gradient identity.
- Keep SVGs lightweight and accessible when used as meaningful images; mark decorative uses `aria-hidden` or empty alt.
- Any AI/Recraft evolution must be treated as a new candidate source and visually inspected before replacing these masters.

## Art direction relationship

The SVG system is subordinate to real founder/client photography. Its purpose is to create a recognizable MACS connective language between real images, not to replace human proof with abstract technology graphics.
